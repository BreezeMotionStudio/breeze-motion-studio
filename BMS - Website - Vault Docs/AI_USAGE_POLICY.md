## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how, when, and under what controls artificial intelligence may be used in any project I produce at Breeze Motion Studio. It exists to:

- Protect the integrity of the AI-FREE VERIFICATION STAMP and AI-FREE VERIFICATION GOLD STANDARD stamp, both licensed to Breeze Motion Studio by AI-FREE MEDIA VERIFICATION AGENCY (Pty) Ltd.
- Give clients a clear, honest, and consistent answer to "was AI used in my project?" every time.
- Set a default (AI-free) and a documented, deliberate exception process, rather than leaving AI use to individual judgment call by call.

This is an internal operating policy first. A shorter public-facing version may be published on the website later, once this document is finalized and reconciled with the agency's official criteria.

---

## 2. Position Statement

Breeze Motion Studio is **not an AI-first company**. AI is not a default tool in my creative process, and it is never used to replace craft, real production, or real footage.

By default, every project I complete contains **no AI-generated content in the final delivered product**. AI is only introduced when a project specifically requires it, for a specific and limited purpose, under the controls in Section 6, with full transparency to the client.

---

## 3. Definitions

**AI-generated content** — Any element of a final deliverable that was created, synthesized, or substantially generated by a generative AI system, rather than captured, filmed, photographed, or manually produced. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Generative image or video content (AI-generated visuals, backgrounds, or footage)
- AI-generated 3D models, environments, or effects standing in for real production or licensed 3D/VFX software output
- Synthetic voice, voice cloning, or AI-altered speech
- Deepfakes or AI-generated human likenesses of any kind
- AI-written copy or script that ships in the final deliverable as-is

**Incidental AI (not counted as AI-generated content)** — See Section 4. Standard production and editing tools with built-in machine-learning features that assist rather than generate.

**Final-product basis (AI-FREE VERIFICATION STAMP)** — The delivered final media product contains no AI-generated content, per the definition above. AI may still have been used elsewhere in the process (see Section 5.2).

**Production basis (AI-FREE VERIFICATION GOLD STANDARD)** — No AI usage of any kind, at any stage, touched this project — from brief to delivery. Elite tier.

**Real environment** — Any physical location, physical set, or physical backdrop captured on camera.

**Digital environment** — A background, backdrop, or environment that is itself computer-generated (e.g. a 3D-rendered set, motion graphics background, or virtual set) rather than a real, physical location.

---

## 4. Incidental AI — What Doesn't Count

⚠ *This carve-out is the single most important part of this policy to reconcile against the agency's official criteria once available. Modern production tools almost universally include ML-based features; without a clear line here, no project could ever qualify for either stamp.*

The following are **not** treated as "AI usage" for the purposes of either stamp, because they assist an existing human-made or human-captured asset rather than generate new content:

- In-camera autofocus, image stabilization, exposure/white-balance processing
- Built-in denoise/noise-reduction in editing software (e.g. Resolve Neural Engine, Lightroom denoise)
- Auto color-match / scene-referred color tools used as a starting point, then manually graded
- ML-assisted audio cleanup (e.g. de-noise, de-reverb) applied to real, on-set recorded audio
- Autocorrect, spell-check, and similar writing-assist features used while drafting internal notes or scripts based on human-authored content
- Business/admin software with embedded AI features (accounting, project management, email) that has no bearing on the creative deliverable
- AI used only for brainstorming, ideation, or as a sounding board during pre-production, provided no AI output ships in the deliverable
- AI used for backend coding, automation, or systems work unrelated to the creative deliverable itself (e.g. this website's own tooling)

These uses **are permitted under AI-FREE VERIFICATION STAMP** without disclosure, but **disqualify a project from GOLD STANDARD**, because Gold Standard requires zero AI usage at any point in the project (see Section 5.2). I still track which of these were used per project internally (Appendix B), so the Gold Standard determination is always accurate.

## 5. The Two Verification Tiers

### 5.1 AI-FREE VERIFICATION STAMP (Final-Product Based)

**Default tier.** Awarded when:

- The final delivered media product contains zero AI-generated content as defined in Section 3.
- Any incidental AI (Section 4) used during production is logged internally but does not need client disclosure.
- Any deliberate, in-scope AI usage (Section 6) that *did* touch the final product has been disclosed to and signed off by the client per Section 8, and is documented in Appendix B.

### 5.2 AI-FREE VERIFICATION GOLD STANDARD (Production Based, Elite)

Awarded when:

- No AI usage of any kind — including incidental AI (Section 4) — touched any stage of the project, from initial brief through final delivery.
- No AI brainstorming, no AI-assisted brief drafting, no ML-based editing tools, nothing.
- This is verified against the project's Appendix B checklist before the Gold Standard stamp is applied.

**Decision rule:** If Appendix B shows *any* checked item (incidental or deliberate), the project cannot carry the Gold Standard stamp — it may still carry the standard AI-FREE VERIFICATION STAMP if no AI reached the final deliverable.

### 5.3 Neither Stamp

If AI-generated content (Section 3) ships in the final deliverable and the client has been informed and has signed the Appendix A disclosure (Section 8), the project carries **neither stamp**. This is not a failure state — it is simply outside the scope of AI-free certification, and is disclosed to the client as such at the point AI use is proposed, not after delivery.

---

## 6. Permitted Scope of Deliberate AI Use

When a project specifically requires AI and cannot reasonably achieve the intended result otherwise, its use is restricted to:

- **Effects added to real, camera-captured footage or photography**, in place of 3D modeling or dedicated effects software — never in place of the camera work itself.
- **Digital environments only.** AI may be used to generate or enhance a digital (computer-generated) environment or backdrop. It is never used to alter, enhance, or replace a real, physical environment that was filmed or photographed.
- **Products are always real.** A physical product is never AI-generated or AI-altered. If a product appears alongside AI-generated effects, the product itself is photographed in full detail with a real camera, and only the surrounding effect is AI-assisted.

## 7. Absolute Prohibitions

Regardless of client request, project type, or budget, Breeze Motion Studio does **not** produce:

- Deepfakes, or any AI-generated likeness of a real person
- AI-generated human replicas or synthetic humans standing in for real people
- AI alteration or enhancement of real, physical filmed/photographed environments
- AI-generated or AI-altered products (see Section 6)
- Synthetic voice or voice-cloning of any real person without that person's separate, explicit written consent (in addition to the client disclosure in Section 8)

---

## 8. Client Disclosure & Consent Process ⚠ PENDING LEGAL REVIEW

**Timing:** Client consent is obtained **before** deliberate AI use is implemented, not only disclosed at delivery. If a project's brief or a mid-project change would require AI under Section 6, I raise it with the client and get sign-off before proceeding.

**Process:**

1. I identify the specific, limited AI use required and confirm it fits Section 6 (permitted scope) and does not fall under Section 7 (prohibited).
2. I explain to the client what will be AI-assisted, why, and what stamp status the project will therefore carry (Section 5.3, if applicable).
3. The client signs the **AI Usage Disclosure & Consent Form** (Appendix A) before the AI-assisted work proceeds.
4. The signed form is retained by Breeze Motion Studio as a permanent internal record (see Section 9).
5. If the client declines, the project proceeds without AI, and remains eligible for AI-FREE VERIFICATION STAMP (or Gold Standard, if no incidental AI was used either).

**Form must state, at minimum:** which specific element(s) of the final product involve AI, which tool/system was used, the reason AI was necessary, confirmation that the product/environment/human-likeness restrictions in Sections 6–7 were respected, and the client's signature and date.

---

## 9. Internal Documentation & Audit Trail

For every project, I complete the **Internal Project AI Checklist** (Appendix B) covering:

- Every tool used in the project (camera, editing software, any AI system)
- Whether each falls under incidental AI (Section 4) or deliberate AI (Section 6)
- The resulting stamp determination (5.1 / 5.2 / 5.3)
- Reference to the signed Appendix A form, if one exists for this project

This record is kept on file for the life of the project relationship and is the evidence I rely on if AI-FREE MEDIA VERIFICATION AGENCY (Pty) Ltd, or a client, ever queries a stamp.

**Revocation/correction:** If a project's stamp status is found to be incorrect after the fact (e.g. an undisclosed tool is later found to use generative AI), I correct the record, notify the client if their project's stamp status changes, and do not re-use that stamp on future marketing of that project until resolved.
